  • What they need: type of job, budget, deadline and location, taken only from what the post actually says.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does it search Facebook automatically?

No, and be wary of anything that says it does. Since 2024 Facebook hasn't allowed any outside app to read group posts, and tools that claim otherwise usually scrape, which breaks Facebook's rules and can get your account restricted.

ClientHunter takes the honest route: a browser helper. While you're reading a group yourself, you highlight a post and send it over with one click — one post, chosen by you, about as automated as copy-and-paste. It arrives scored, checked and with a reply ready to edit. The same button works on WhatsApp Web, Nextdoor, LinkedIn and any other site.

Will it message people for me?

Never. ClientHunter writes a draft; you read it, edit it and send it yourself. Automatic messages get accounts banned and put clients off, and it's not how good work is won.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once. Your licence includes 12 months of updates, and after that you keep using the version you have, for as long as you like.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 or 11 computer (64-bit). That's it. The AI is built in: one click in Settings downloads it (about 1.1 GB, once) and it then runs on your own machine, offline and free. No accounts, no command line, no monthly bill. If you'd rather use your own Claude or OpenAI account, you can.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your profiles, leads and notes are stored on your own computer, not on our servers. With the free local AI, nothing about your leads leaves your machine.

Windows showed a warning when I installed it. Is that normal?

Yes. ClientHunter is made by an independent developer and doesn't have a paid Microsoft signing certificate yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Does it work for trades like painting or plumbing?

Yes. Set your service and travel distance and it only shows jobs in your area. Today most trade leads come from posts you paste in from local Facebook groups, WhatsApp and Nextdoor. Reddit and more local sources are on the way.

How do I get my licence key?

After you buy, your licence key is sent to you by email. Paste it into Settings → Licence in the app. No internet connection is needed to activate.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. It's planned. The trial is Windows-only for now.
